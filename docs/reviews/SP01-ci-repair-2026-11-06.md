  Playwright package's supported dependencies. Retain every e2e step and timeout.
- Replace the route module's `os` binding in the upload regression test rather
  than mutating global `os.fstat`. Keep `fstat.assert_not_called()` and native
  HANDLE identity/CloseHandle assertions for matching and changed directories.

  namespace rather than patching a dynamically created MagicMock child.

  shared approval-button fixtures. Give the dynamically loaded adapter a local

  Authorization, owner IDs and unresolved approval assertions remain unchanged.
